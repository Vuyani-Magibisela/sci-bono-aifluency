About the Sci-Bono AI Hub

Sci-Bono Discovery Centre's ICT contribution to empowering South Africa with AI knowledge and skills — open to anyone ready to learn, build, and make an impact.

What is the AI Hub?

The Sci-Bono AI Hub is a free, open learning platform created by the ICT department at Sci-Bono Discovery Centre in Johannesburg. It exists to give every South African — learner, student, educator, professional, or curious mind — a clear pathway into artificial intelligence, from understanding the basics all the way to building and running AI agents.

More than a course catalogue, the platform is a living repository of AI learning resources. Whether you are hearing the term "machine learning" for the first time or you are ready to deploy your own project, there is a place for you here.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To democratise AI education in South Africa by providing accessible, project-based learning that equips people with the knowledge and hands-on skills to harness artificial intelligence for personal growth and community development.

Our Vision

A South Africa where every community has people who understand, use, and build with AI — turning technology into a tool for opportunity, innovation, and positive change.

How We Teach

The AI Hub is built on the belief that you learn AI by doing AI. Every module pairs theory with real-world application, and every course culminates in a hands-on project. Participants don't just absorb information — they build things, solve problems, and implement what they learn to better themselves and their communities.

Project-Based

Every course requires participants to get their hands dirty building real projects that apply AI concepts.

Progressive Depth

Start with AI basics, advance through generative AI, prompt engineering, and work your way up to building and running agents.

Community Impact

Projects are designed to solve real problems — encouraging participants to use AI to uplift their communities.

Open to All

No prerequisites, no fees. If you have curiosity and internet access, you can start learning today.

Who Is It For?

The AI Hub is for anyone interested in learning about AI. Our community includes:

  • High-school learners (Grades 8–12) — getting a head start on the technology shaping their future careers.
  • University students — supplementing their studies with practical AI skills and portfolio-ready projects.
  • Educators and trainers — building AI literacy so they can bring these skills into their own classrooms.
  • Working professionals — upskilling to stay relevant in an AI-driven job market.
  • Curious minds — anyone who wants to understand what AI is, how it works, and what they can do with it.

About Sci-Bono Discovery Centre

Sci-Bono Discovery Centre is South Africa's largest science centre, located in Newtown, Johannesburg. As a Gauteng Department of Education initiative, Sci-Bono is dedicated to advancing science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) education across the province.

The AI Hub is an extension of that mission — bringing the ICT department's expertise in digital skills training to a wider audience through an accessible online platform focused entirely on artificial intelligence.
